## Session 1 — Where we are, and whether it is real

- Recap after two days away. Everything committed and pushed; the Render site is live and
  redeploys on every push.
- Owner's questions: is the plan good, is it achievable with this stack, can the film room's
  output really come from video alone? Answers written up in the chat and in the decision log's
  spirit: yes with named risks; field registration is the one that decides it.
- Fact from the owner: All-22 film shows no painted first-down or scrimmage lines. Registration
  now anchors on the painted yard numbers plus the official line of scrimmage, two independent
  anchors that must agree.
- New process: a "Right now" block on the Now page, fed by a small file and a one-line command
  that logs a step and pushes it, so the site moves in baby steps during a session rather than
  at the end of one.
